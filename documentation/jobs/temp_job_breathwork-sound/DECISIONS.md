# Decisions — temp_job_breathwork-sound

| # | Decision | Alternatives considered | Why this one |
|---|----------|-------------------------|--------------|
| D11 | Web Audio API directly. No new package, no copied code. | Tone.js (new dependency); importing 1ps0/binaural (an app, not a library, with globals) | Binaural beats need two oscillators and a channel merger, which Web Audio already offers. Owner asked for modifiable parameters, which a small own module gives. |
| D12 | Beat per phase: inhale ramps from beat-low to beat-high across the inhale; exhale ramps back down; hold stays at beat-low. | Fixed beat; beat only in holds | The owner asked for a beat that follows the breath. Holding at the low value means the beat stops moving during the hold, like the circle. |
| D13 | Defaults: carrier 200 Hz, beat-low 4 Hz, beat-high 10 Hz. Bounds: carrier 100–400 Hz, beat 1–40 Hz, beat-low ≤ beat-high. | Carrier and beat fixed at 1ps0's values | 200 Hz is the carrier used in 1ps0's README and the 2024 preprint compared 250/400 Hz. The beat range is the theta-like band most content uses. Bounds keep the tone audible (above ~20 Hz is a beat on a carrier). |
| D14 | Headphones mode: left oscillator at carrier, right at carrier + beat, merged to separate channels. Speakers mode: one carrier tone amplitude-modulated at the beat rate by an LFO (isochronic-like). | Only headphones mode; speakers mode as a pulse train | The owner wants it to work without headphones. Amplitude modulation keeps the beat audible on speakers without timers. The pulse train would need timers, which the audio path forbids. |
| D15 | Overtone layers are not part of this lap. The hold uses a subtle beat at half gain. Sound state and audio settings stay in page state. | Overtones now; persistence now | Scope: owner asked for binaural beats first. Subtle hold keeps the design's "silent or subtle" rule. |
